WHAT DOES THE EXPLANATION SCORE REWARD?

89.3% of the gain comes
from recognizing success.

The benchmark’s composite ROUGE-L rises from 0.185 to 0.499. Most of the increase is in the correct-success component; the score is not a percentage of correct failure explanations.

How the composite score is computed

Correct success → 1.
Correct failure → stemmed ROUGE-L F1 against the reference explanation.
Wrong label → 0.
Average across all 57 questions.

ROUGE-L measures lexical overlap. It does not verify a physical cause. The failure component also depends on which failures are detected.

07 / INTERPRETING THE EVIDENCE

Keep the measurements
attached to their questions.

01

Correlation ≠ completion

VOC describes a trend against time. High VOC can coexist with a wrong terminal estimate. Time-based MSE is not a human semantic-completion label.

02

Accuracy ≠ failure recall

A gain in overall accuracy can hide more missed failures. Report both error directions before discussing intervention quality.

03

Offline evidence ≠ deployment

There is no demonstrated online localization, causal diagnosis, recovery control, or improvement in robot learning in this report.

Evaluation conditions & distinct RoboFail adaptations +

Inputs and examples

Progress: five observed-prefix positions; one same- or similar-task training trajectory with six stage scores for ICL. Ranking: eight test frames; three random high/middle/low training references. Direct preference: both trajectories in one request.

RoboFail: original montage and question. Five fixed external image–question–answer demonstrations are reported disjoint by test trajectory. This does not establish environment, embodiment, near-duplicate, or pretraining independence.

What remains unresolved

Questions cluster in 15 sequences, and ranking pairs are dependent. Repeated-call variation and confidence intervals are unavailable. Requested model names do not establish backend equivalence or equal token budgets.

Media replays and model outputs are drawn from saved evaluations. They introduce no new aggregate benchmark scores.

Guardian and PRIMO are different evaluation supports

Guardian uses 153 execution / 30 planning examples; PRIMO uses 100 Cosmos-curated examples. The report includes their published results separately. No Astra score is available on either adaptation. In the Guardian table, +GF denotes training on GuardianFail-36k.
